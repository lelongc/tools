Are you using the word get correctly? 
 
Get is a versatile verb in English, used to receive or obtain something, get a job, get married, or get a gift.
It can also mean to become or feel something, like get tired, get happy, or get excited.

Get is often used in phrasal verbs, like get up, get on, or get off, which can be confusing for learners.
For example, get up means to stand up or wake up, while get on means to board a vehicle or continue with something.

To get the most out of this verb, practice using it in different contexts, like get a book from the library, get a message from a friend, or get a feeling about something.
Get comfortable with get, and your English will get better.
