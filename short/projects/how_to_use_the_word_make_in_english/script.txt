What does the word make mean to you? 
   It can mean to create something, like a craft project, or to force someone to do something. 
   
   For example, you can make a cake, or make someone laugh. You can also make a decision, or make a change in your life. 
   
   By using make in different contexts, you can express a wide range of ideas and actions. Making something can be fun, like crafting or cooking. Making a decision can be difficult, but it's a necessary part of life.
