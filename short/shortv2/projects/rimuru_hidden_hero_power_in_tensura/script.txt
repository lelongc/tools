Most fans know Rimuru as a powerful Demon Lord. But did you know he secretly possesses the power to become a True Hero. This duality is rare in history.

Rimuru holds the unique ability to wield both demonic and holy energy simultaneously. While his evolution into a Demon Lord is public knowledge, his hidden potential as a Hero is hidden behind his complex skill sets. By manipulating the Harvest Festival essence, Rimuru bypassed traditional limitations. He fused opposing forces within his core, effectively becoming a bridge between two worlds. This balance grants him immunity to holy magic that would destroy any other fiend. It is a terrifying secret that keeps his enemies guessing. Most gods fear this specific combination of divinity and malice.

Could this secret identity be the key to his ultimate victory. The world of Tensura is far deeper than it appears. Are you ready for what comes next.
