Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about how NeoClip works.

🤔

Why did it miss a copied item?

Chrome's strict security sandbox prevents extensions from secretly spying on your clipboard in the background. If you copy multiple items outside the browser (like in Word), NeoClip can only catch the last one when you switch back.

Pro Tip: Copy directly inside Chrome, or use Win + V to manually paste older items into NeoClip!

☁️

Where is my data stored?

We literally do not own a database. Your clipboard history stays locally on your computer. If you enable Cloud Sync, it is securely stored in a hidden folder inside your own Google Drive.

⚠️ Important Trade-off: Because data is stored locally, if you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data without syncing to Google Drive, your history will be lost permanently. We recommend Pro for Cloud Sync!

🔍

How does image text extraction work?

NeoClip opens your copied images in a lightweight Google Lens panel. This allows you to leverage Google's world-class AI to extract text, translate, or find similar images instantly.

⚡

Does Auto-Paste work everywhere?

When you click an item to Auto-Paste, NeoClip uses your operating system's native clipboard to paste it. Some highly secure websites may block automated pasting. If nothing happens, NeoClip will show a friendly reminder to simply press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V) to manually paste!

💳

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Check your email for the receipt sent by Lemon Squeezy when you upgraded. It contains a Manage Subscription link leading to your Customer Portal. There, you can update your payment method, download invoices, or cancel anytime!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NeoClip.

Where is my clipboard data stored?

100% locally on your device! We don't have a database. If you upgrade to Pro and enable Cloud Sync, your data is securely backed up to a hidden folder inside your personal Google Drive.

Can I use my Pro license on multiple computers?

Yes! NeoClip uses a "1 Drive = 1 Key" licensing model. As long as you log into the same Google Drive account, your Pro license and clipboard data will seamlessly sync across all your devices.

What happens if my Pro subscription expires?

Your existing data is never deleted! You can still view all your saved clips. However, new saves will be restricted to the Free tier limits (50 items, 3 collections). Your locked collections will show a 🔒 icon but remain safe until you renew.

How does the Image Text Extraction (OCR) work?

We integrated seamlessly with Google Lens. When you copy an image, simply click the "Extract Text" button in the extension, and it instantly opens a Google Lens panel to copy text from that image.
