2. USE CASE CHINH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vi du: Copy text tu Zalo -> Paste vao Facebook dang bai + chon anh:
  Step 1: Bat bong bong noi -> Bam 🔴 REC
  Step 2: Mo Zalo -> Nhan giu tin nhan -> Copy text
  Step 3: Chuyen sang Facebook -> Bam "Ban dang nghi gi?" -> Dan text
  Step 4: Bam chon anh -> Chon anh tu thu vien
  Step 5: Bam Dang -> Bam ⏹️ STOP tren bong bong
  Step 6: Dat ten hanh dong "Copy Zalo Paste Facebook"
  Step 7: Lan sau chi can bam ▶️ Chay hoac ⏰ Dat lich tu dong

3. CO CHE KY THUAT RECORDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dung Android Accessibility Service (khong can Root)
- Bong bong noi (Floating Overlay Widget) dung WindowManager
- Quy trinh ghi 1 thao tac:
  a. Overlay toan man hinh (trong suot) bat su kien cham cua user
  b. Ghi nhan toa do (x, y), loai cu chi (TAP/SWIPE/LONG_PRESS), 
     thoi gian giu va DELAY THUC TE giua cac thao tac
  c. Tam tat overlay (FLAG_NOT_TOUCHABLE) de touch xuyen qua app phia duoi
  d. Dung dispatchGesture() cua AccessibilityService inject cu chi vao app
  e. Sau khi inject xong, bat lai overlay de bat thao tac tiep theo
- Playback: Chay lai toan bo chuoi thao tac voi timing chinh xac
